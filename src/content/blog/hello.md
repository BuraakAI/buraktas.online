Hi, I'm Burak. In this corner I write about what I build where **AI, autonomous systems and agricultural technology** meet — what I learn, and where I get stuck.

For me, building is a way of working: take an idea and carry it through hardware, software and field testing until it becomes a **working product**. The notes here are the raw log of that journey — not polished essays, but real **ship logs**.

## What you'll find

- **Project logs** — SeraOS, AgriVision and what comes next: what I built and why.
- **Short technical notes** — things I learn about ESP32, sensors, computer vision and LoRa.
- **Experiments** — the ones that work, and the ones that don't.

> Good engineering turns chaos into discipline.

I'm starting here. More to come. 🐇
